# Yahya Ali Alali

**PhD Candidate in Computer Engineering**

I am a PhD candidate in Computer Engineering at Istanbul University-Cerrahpaşa. I investigate adaptive routing and quality of service in hybrid SDN–MPLS networks. PPO is studied for route selection, and LSTM-based forecasting is an auxiliary component. I also develop software systems and write technical material in Arabic.

[Intelligent routing and quality of service in SDN–MPLS networks](https://github.com/yaheaali92/project-showcase/blob/main/case-studies/phd-routing/en.md) · [Doctoral research overview (ongoing)](https://yahyaalali.com/en/research/phd-routing/)
